Cabin rentals

Somewhere quiet, with a view

Two private cabins on the edge of the farm, a short drive from Digby and the ferry.

Cabin one

Breezy Hill Bunkie

A compact, comfortable cabin for two, with everything you need and nothing you do not. The bed sits under a wall of warm cedar panelling, and the window looks out over the trees toward the water.

The best part is outside. There is a private patio with proper lounge chairs and a barbecue, positioned so that you can sit with a drink and watch the light move across the basin. On a clear evening it is hard to beat.

  • Sleeps two
  • Comfortable double bed
  • Walk-in shower and washroom
  • Kitchenette with fridge and microwave
  • Private patio and barbecue
  • Views over the Annapolis Basin

Cabin two

Copper's Cabin

A little more room to spread out, and named after the farm dog, who considers the whole property his and will very likely come and introduce himself.

Inside it is bright and simple. There is a proper queen bed, a full washroom with a tub, and a small table set against the window where you can have your coffee and look straight down the valley. Outside, the covered deck means you can sit out even when the Bay of Fundy weather has other ideas.

  • Sleeps two
  • Queen bed
  • Full washroom with bath and shower
  • Kitchenette with fridge and coffee maker
  • Dining table by the window
  • Covered deck with seating

While you are here

What is on the doorstep

The farm trails

Guests are welcome to walk the trails on the property whenever they like. It is a good way to stretch your legs first thing, and the views from the top of the hill are the reason people keep coming back.

Digby and the ferry

Digby is a short drive away, with restaurants, the waterfront and the scallop fleet. The ferry across to Saint John leaves from there, which makes the cabins a handy overnight stop either side of a crossing.

The Annapolis Basin

The whole basin is right there below you. Beaches, lookoffs, Digby Neck and the whale watching boats are all within easy reach for a day out.

Ask about a stay

Tell us which cabin you like the look of and roughly when you are hoping to come. We will get back to you with availability and a price, which will be lower than the same dates on Airbnb because there is no platform fee in the middle.

If you are not sure which cabin suits, say so and we will help you pick. And if your dates are flexible, mention that too, because it often means we can do better for you.
