About our opening hours

We do not have set hours to publish. Farm days depend on the weather, the season and what needs doing, so rather than post times we cannot keep to, we let people know on Facebook when the farm is open, when a workshop is running and which markets we will be at.

If you would like to come up and see the place, just send us a message and we will sort out a time that works.

Before you write

A few quick answers

Can I order online?

No, and that is deliberate. Plants are living things, and a tray that looks perfect one day can be sold out or simply not ready the next. We would rather not take your money for something we cannot be sure we can hand over. Ask us what we have and we will hold it for you.

Can I just turn up?

Have a look at Facebook first to see whether we are about, or send a quick message. We are usually somewhere on the property, but a heads up means somebody will be there to meet you rather than halfway down a field.

Are the cabins farm stays?

No. They are ordinary holiday rentals that happen to be on a farm. No chores and no obligations. You are welcome to walk the trails and see what is growing, but only if you feel like it.

Is it worth booking direct?

Yes. Both cabins are on Airbnb so people can find them, but booking through us means there is no platform fee, so the same stay costs you less.

Can children come?

Absolutely. There are ducks to look at, a friendly dog, and plenty of room to run about. Just keep an eye on little ones around the growing beds and the equipment.

Do you do bouquets for occasions?

We do, within reason and within the season. Let us know what you have in mind and roughly when, and we will tell you honestly what we can manage.
